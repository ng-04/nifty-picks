Which of your holdings to sell

A Sell needs all three together: the 50-day average below the 200-day average, the price below its 200-day average, and 12-1 momentum in the bottom quarter of the Nifty 500. From 2019 to 2026 such stocks trailed everything else in 7 of 8 years: about 1.7 points less return per quarter, and a 24% chance of falling 10% or more against 19%. Review = momentum in the bottom 10% or a technical score under 25. Keep = on the buy list or in the top 20.

The evidence is modest, and low model scores alone did not predict losses. A verdict is a prompt to decide, not an order. Selling a gain can create capital-gains tax and selling a loss can offset gains. ETFs are not rated.

How the list is built

  • Universe. Current Nifty 500 constituents with market cap of at least ₹5,000 Cr, from Yahoo Finance daily prices since 2016.
  • Technical score (70%). Average of two percentiles: a gradient-boosted model's chance of a 5% gain within 63 sessions, using all 42 algorithms plus market trend and breadth; and a vote of the eight algorithms that tested best on past data. Both smoothed over about 10 sessions.
  • Graham score (30%). P/E ≤ 15, P/B ≤ 1.5, price below the Graham number √(22.5 × EPS × book value), current ratio ≥ 2, low debt, profits every reported year, EPS growth, unbroken dividends, and P/E and P/B well below their own recent peak. Current ratio and debt are skipped for lenders.
  • List rules. Ten best combined scores, at most three per NSE industry. A listed stock stays until it falls below rank 20. New = entered today; Downgraded = score down 5+ points or rank down 5+ places since entry; Dropped = left today.
  • Privacy. The data files on this site are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds). Decryption happens only in your browser.

What to keep in mind

  • Chance figures are model estimates, not promises. The Backtest tab shows how often top picks actually reached +5%.
  • Only the technical layer is backtested. Survivorship bias inflates backtest returns. Taxes are ignored.
  • Yahoo Finance data can be late or wrong for Indian stocks. Check a price before acting on it.
